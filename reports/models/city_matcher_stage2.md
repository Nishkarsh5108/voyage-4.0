# Second-stage (collective) matcher on the 12-city benchmark

Generated by `scripts/train/city_matcher_stage2.py`. Same **weak labels** and the same leave-one-city-out protocol as `reports/models/city_matcher.md`; stage 1 below is that report's model, unchanged.

Stage 2 adds collective features computed from stage-1 scores: each record's best competing candidate from the same partner source (a record has at most one twin per source), its rank among them, how many strong candidates it has, and two-hop support through a third record. Every stage-1 score that feeds stage 2 comes from a booster that never saw the held-out city: 132 nested boosters (without the held-out city and without the scored training city) supply the training rows.

## Why recall at 99% precision fell in dubai

The labelled "non-matches" of dubai that stage 2 scores highest. A non-match label here means the two records carry different Wikidata items (`qid_conflict`) or different phones and domains. Read the names:

|---:|---:|---:|---|---|---|

| 0.99 | 0.953 | 78 | qid_conflict | wikidata: Wafi Mall | osm: وافي مول |

| 0.973 | 0.967 | 72 | qid_conflict | wikidata: Fairmont Dubai | osm: Fairmont Dubai |

Most of these are one place under two Wikidata items, often a Latin name in one source and the Arabic name in another. Stage 2 ranks them higher because two-hop support links the scripts, and the weak labels then count them as false positives at the top of the ranking, which is the one place recall at 99% precision is measured. The human label sheet is what settles it.

## Reading the numbers

- The labels are weak (shared Wikidata QID, or rare phone / URL plus proximity), as in the stage-1 report. Both stages are scored against the same labels, so the difference between the columns is a fair comparison; the absolute values still need the human label sheet.
- Recall at a fixed 99% precision hinges on a handful of top-scored non-matches and moves by several points between runs; average precision and AUC are the steadier measures.
- The website still runs stage 1. Stage 2 needs stage-1 scores for every candidate in a city before it can score any pair, which fits the batch pipeline; wiring it into the live preset runs is listed in PROGRESS.md.
