Same 87 real Klook Singapore products and the same open-data gazetteer as `activities.md`, but a second synthetic supplier written with **new templates** after the rules were frozen (different seed, new noise, synonyms, attribute phrasings, two attribute families the in-sample set never used, four new combo formats). ## Reading the numbers

- Still a synthetic second supplier, so this measures robustness to unseen phrasings, not accuracy on a real feed.
- The gap to the in-sample score is the honest size of the lexicon's blind spots; the template rows show which phrasings the lexicon has no word for (they go to the model stage in the cascade).
