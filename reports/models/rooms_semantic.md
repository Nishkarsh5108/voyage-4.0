Generated by `scripts/train/rooms_semantic_baseline.py` on the exact renamed strings of `reports/models/rooms.md` (50,310 strings, 1,484 real hotels). Same task: map each renamed string to the right room of its own hotel, or abstain. Cosine floors and margins are picked on 20% of the hotels (287) for the most correct mappings at 99% precision, and every number below is on the other 80%.

## Reading the numbers

- Same caveat as the parser benchmark: the second supplier is simulated by rewrite rules, so this measures robustness to renaming.
- A sentence embedding has to put `Superior Twin Room` far from `Superior Double Room` and close to `SUP TWN`; the sibling rooms of one hotel are the distractors, which is exactly where general-purpose embeddings are weakest.
- The cascade row is the design in the architecture: deterministic attributes first, a learned matcher only for what they leave.
